Self Help Books • Key Points & Implementation

T. Harv Eker — Secrets of the Millionaire Mind

Personal blueprint: use the book's wealth mindset principles as a practical operating system: replace inherited scarcity patterns with ownership, take responsibility for outcomes, increase earning capacity through value creation, deliberately allocate income, accumulate assets, and review behaviour every week.

01 • Money blueprint

Write down old beliefs about money, identify which ones limit action, and replace them with evidence-based statements about ownership, learning and value creation.

02 • Responsibility

When a result is below target, ask “What can I change?” before blaming the market, employer, economy or other people.

03 • Income growth

Build high-value skills in IT, AI, media, consulting and business so income can rise without relying on one job.

04 • Wealth allocation

Separate money for necessities, long-term wealth, learning, giving and controlled enjoyment instead of treating all cash as spendable.

05 • Assets first

Prioritise investments, business systems, intellectual property and productive equipment that can create future cash flow.

06 • Multiple streams

Develop complementary streams: IT consulting, content/media, logistics, tourism/property and investments.

Adams Nomi • Weekly Wealth Operating Rhythm

  • Monday: review cash position and commitments.
  • Mid-week: execute one income-growth action.
  • Friday: move planned savings/investment money.
  • Weekend: review goal progress and remove one bottleneck.
  • Monthly: calculate net worth, income by source and asset growth.
  • Quarterly: kill weak projects, double down on high-value opportunities.

North Star

Use money to buy freedom, productive assets, capability and impact — not only lifestyle.
